# Life OS: Official User Guide

Welcome to the Life OS User Guide. This document provides practical instructions and real-world scenarios on how to best utilize the V1.0.0 suite to systematically upgrade your life.

---

## 1. Daily Planner

**Purpose:** Map out your day with precision to avoid the anxiety of a chaotic schedule and protect your deep work hours.

**How to Use:**

1. Navigate to **Daily Planner** from the sidebar.
2. In the "Task Pool" on the left, type the name of a task. To assign a specific time duration, add a comma followed by the number of hours (e.g., `Write marketing copy, 2`).
3. Drag the task block from the pool and drop it onto the timeline on the right (e.g., at `09:00`). The timeline will automatically reserve 2 hours, blocking out the space until `11:00`.
4. Click the small checkbox circle to mark it as completed as you progress.
5. Watch your "Planned Time" and "Completed Tasks" metrics update at the top of the timeline. Use the "Daily Review" moon icon at the very end of your day.

**Example Scenario:**
You have a 4-hour open block in the afternoon. Instead of winging it, you create two tasks: `Frontend Development, 2.5` and `Team Call, 1`. You drop them onto the timeline starting at `13:00`. You now have a concrete, visually enforced plan that prevents you from endlessly scrolling social media.

## 2. Pomodoro Timer

**Purpose:** Use cyclical sprint sessions to execute the high-leverage tasks you assigned in your Daily Planner.

**How to Use:**

1. Navigate to **Pomodoro**.
2. Press "START" and plunge into absolute deep work for 25 minutes.
3. When the bell rings, the timer will automatically transition to a 5-minute Rest state. Walk away from your desk.
4. If you need a more aggressive session, modify the focus and rest durations in the upper-right settings icon (e.g., configure it for 50 min focus / 10 min rest).

**Example Scenario:**
Your Daily Planner shows a 2-hour task block for "Architecture Diagram." You open the Pomodoro, start the timer, and execute 4 consecutive Pomodoros to complete the task with razor-sharp intensity.

## 3. Water Reminder

**Purpose:** Ensure you do not suffer a physical crash due to dehydration while focusing.

**How to Use:**

1. Navigate to **Water Reminder**.
2. Start the tracking cycle.
3. Every 60 minutes, the app will aggressively take over your screen, fading your work out and prompting you to drink water.
4. Drink a glass of water, click "Mark as done," and the app will release control back to you.

**Example Scenario:**
You get lost in a massive flow state while programming. Three hours pass. Without the Water Reminder, you would wake up with a headache. Because the reminder interrupted your screen every hour, you drank 3 full cups of water naturally, maintaining your peak physiological state.

## 4. Health Dashboard

**Purpose:** Measure your "Energy Engine" objectively to understand correlations between your physical health and mental performance.

**How to Use:**

1. Open the **Health Dashboard**.
2. In the Daily Log card, enter today's metric (Weight in lbs, Sleep in hours, Calories consumed, and Water in oz) and click "Log Data".
3. Over the span of weeks, observe the generated line charts beneath the input panel.

**Example Scenario:**
You feel sluggish and unable to focus. You pull up the Health Dashboard and observe a massive downward trend in the Sleep chart over the last 4 days. You identify the culprit for your fatigue and prioritize sleeping 8 hours tonight.

## 5. Self-Awareness

**Purpose:** Guarantee the goals you are grinding toward every day actually align with who you are and what you want out of life.

**How to Use:**

1. Open **Self-Awareness** in the Life section.
2. Navigate through the tabs across the top (Vision, Mission, Values, Strengths, Weaknesses, Skills, Reflection).
3. Type raw, honest thoughts into the editor. It auto-saves locally instantly.

**Example Scenario:**
You receive a job offer moving to a different city and dealing with massive stress for more money. You open your "Values" and "Vision" tabs, recognizing you wrote "Prioritize peace and location independence." The answer becomes immediately clear, saving you from a terrible life decision.

## 6. Milestones

**Purpose:** Visually record the major events and victories you experience.

**How to Use:**

1. Go to **Milestones**.
2. Click `+ Add Milestone`. Pick a date/month, choose a compelling title (e.g., "Shipped Life OS V1.0.0"), and write down the context or lessons learned.
3. Click "Save". A beautiful left-bordered card will render indefinitely as a trophy of your hard work.

**Example Scenario:**
It's December, and you feel like you haven't accomplished much this year. You open your Milestones page and see 8 large event cards scrolling down the timeline (Promotions, project launches, relationship milestones). You feel an immense wave of pride.

## 7. Resume Builder

**Purpose:** Turn your day-to-day work into a professional export guaranteed to pass corporate ATS screens.

**How to Use:**

1. Use **Resume Sections** to input your personal details, work experience, massive projects, and education.
2. Go to **Resume Builder**, reorder or customize the specific templates if needed, and preview the generated LaTeX document on the right.
3. Export the beautiful PDF.

**Example Scenario:**
A recruiter asks for your resume. Because you've been incrementally tracking your "Projects" as they happen through the Resume Sections module, you open Life OS, verify the layout, click Export, and send a world-class PDF within 30 seconds.

---

## 8. Settings & Data Control

**Purpose:** Control your local environment.

**How to Use:**

1. Go to the newly unified **Settings** screen via the gear icon.
2. To backup your Life OS system to another PC, click "Export Database (.db)". Keep this file safe.
3. If you want to start fresh or wipe the data, use the "Clear All Data" Danger Zone. All Life OS operations save statically on your local Hard Drive, meaning absolutely no cloud servers have access to your personal journal entries.
